// 03. MATERIAL CARE & MAINTENANCE PROTOCOL

DURABILITY PRESERVATION

REFLECTIVE POLYMER PRESERVATION

To ensure the long-term durability of the tribal reflective high-transfer polymers, wash garments strictly inside out on cold cycles (maximum 30°C / 86°F) using mild neutral detergents. Never use chlorine bleach or industrial enzyme softeners that degrade membrane integrity.

HEAVYWEIGHT DRYING & IRONING

Do not tumble dry high-grammage cotton (300g–400g/m²) or waterproof outer layers to avoid shrinkage or polymer distortion. Hang dry naturally in shaded areas. If ironing is required, apply low temperature on reverse side and strictly avoid direct thermal contact with reflective panels.
